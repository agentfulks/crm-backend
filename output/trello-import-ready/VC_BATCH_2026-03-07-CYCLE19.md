- **Recent Gaming / AI Investments:**

- **Strategic Fit (92/100):** BITKRAFT is actively underwriting AI-enabled production pipelines; Hyve’s proven LiveOps automation gives them immediate proof points on revenue lift and compliant content velocity to pair with their portfolio’s creator tools.
- **Outreach Angles:**

  2. Share anonymized case studies on Hyve’s AAA co-development so welevel’s procedural content teams can offload polish and compliance to Hyve while they focus on AI experimentation.[3]
  3. Offer to pilot Hyve’s moderation + monetization rails with Liminal’s UGC creators, ensuring safe drops that generate incremental ARPU the way Hyve already did for its $150M+ enterprise partner.[2]
