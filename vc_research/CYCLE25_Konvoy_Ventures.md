## Hypothesis of Pain/Opportunity

**The Pain:**
Live ops games require constant content updates, but mid-size studios (20-200 people) lack the engineering resources to build internal tools for event management, A/B testing, and player segmentation. They cobble together spreadsheets, cron jobs, and manual processes.

**The Opportunity:**
A unified live ops platform that plugs into existing game backends and provides AI-powered automation for:
- Event scheduling and management
- Predictive player churn prevention
- Dynamic content personalization
- Automated A/B testing

**Why Konvoy Cares:**
This is exactly their thesis — infrastructure that makes game development more efficient. It's a "pick and shovel" play in the live ops gold rush.
