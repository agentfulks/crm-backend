## 5. Personalized Angle

**Why The Games Fund Would Care About AI-Powered Game Characters/Live Ops:**

TGF has invested in **Layer.ai**, an AI-powered platform for creating professional in-game content, marketing, and live ops art at scale. This demonstrates clear conviction in AI tooling for game development. Their portfolio includes studios building complex multiplayer and narrative-driven games (Dark Passenger, Drama Studios) that require sophisticated character systems and ongoing live operations.

**Angle:** The Games Fund understands that AI is transforming how games are built and operated. Their Layer.ai investment addresses asset creation—AI-powered live characters would complete the loop from creation to runtime. Their Eastern European portfolio studios are building ambitious titles that need this technology to compete globally.
