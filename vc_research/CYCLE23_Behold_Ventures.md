## 3. Investment Thesis

Behold Ventures invests in early-stage video game startups founded by ambitious and creative founders with strategic thinking capabilities. They look for teams "hellbent on creating the most amazing entertainment experiences of tomorrow." The firm takes a "reversed due diligence" approach, encouraging founders to pressure-test investors—a philosophy rooted in their belief that early-stage investments are long journeys requiring aligned travel companions.

**Key Thesis Elements:**
- Deeply founder-centric approach focused on people over products
- Nordic/European gaming ecosystem specialists
- Operational expertise from AAA game development backgrounds
- Strategic LP base includes Krafton, NetEase, and EIF (first-ever games investment)

## 5. Personalized Angle

**Why Behold Ventures Would Care About AI-Powered Game Characters/Live Ops:**

Behold Ventures has invested in **Layer.ai**—an AI-powered art generation platform for game studios—demonstrating active interest in AI tooling for game development. Their portfolio companies are building ambitious titles that require sophisticated live operations (multiplayer action games, RPGs, 4X strategies).

**Angle:** Behold's portfolio companies (Dark Passenger, Roar Games, Zakazane) are building complex multiplayer and narrative-driven games that require dynamic character systems and ongoing live ops. The firm understands from their AAA backgrounds that live operations are make-or-break for modern games—and their investment in Layer.ai shows they're actively hunting for AI tooling that gives studios competitive advantages.

**Specific Hook:** Reference their Layer.ai investment and how AI-powered characters can extend that tooling vision into runtime/live ops rather than just asset creation.
