## 3. Investment Thesis

Play Ventures invests early into the most promising game studios, consumer apps, and related B2B infrastructure & services startups from across the globe. They focus on three core areas:

1. **Games:** Free-to-play and Web3 game studios (mobile and PC)
2. **Playable Apps:** Consumer apps learning from gaming's mastery of UA, economy design, and monetization (Duolingo, Calm-style products)
3. **B2B, AI & Infrastructure:** Adtech, payments infrastructure, and AI transforming how games attract, retain, and monetize users

**Key Thesis Elements:**
- Invest before products exist (founder-first)
- Provide tailored support through unique network (recruitment, design, fundraising)
- Encourage portfolio collaboration
- Focus on hyper-personalization, continuous optimization, frictionless transactions

**Notable:** Winners of Pocket Gamer Mobile Games Award for Investor of the Year (two years in a row)
